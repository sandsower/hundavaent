# Hundavænt

Hundavænt is the shared language for discovering, evaluating, and maintaining trustworthy information about dog-friendly places.
The glossary keeps public discovery, community participation, moderation, and venue participation conceptually distinct.

## Places

**Place**:
A specific independently governed dog-access destination at one Location.
A different destination at the same Location, or the same destination after moving to a new Location, is a distinct Place.
_Avoid_: Venue, business, listing, site

**Location**:
The physical address or bounded outdoor area occupied by a Place.
The same Location may be occupied by different Places over time.
_Avoid_: Place, venue, listing

**Operator**:
The business, organization, or public body responsible for governing a Place.
_Avoid_: Owner, Venue Representative, brand

**Operational Continuity**:
The uninterrupted continuation of the same Place under the same Operator at the same Location.
A name-only rebrand does not end Operational Continuity, while a new Operator or material change of purpose does.
_Avoid_: Same listing, same name

**Contained Place**:
A Place located within another Place while retaining its own dog-access rules.
_Avoid_: Sub-listing, child venue

**Place Profile**:
Hundavænt's public representation of a Place and its verified information.
_Avoid_: Listing, page, site

**Candidate Place**:
A suggested Place awaiting a publication decision and not available through public discovery.
_Avoid_: Draft listing, unverified Place

**Published Place**:
A Place with a public Place Profile and at least one verified Access Condition.
_Avoid_: Active listing, approved Place

**Inactive Place**:
A Place no longer available through normal discovery whose history is retained.
_Avoid_: Deleted Place, expired listing

**Dog Access**:
The verified permission for dogs to be present at a Place, including the applicable Access Conditions.
_Avoid_: Dog-Friendliness, allowed status

**Access Condition**:
A specific circumstance or restriction governing Dog Access, such as an indoor boundary, time limit, leash requirement, or size restriction.
_Avoid_: Feature, tag, rule

**Dog Access Profile**:
The complete set of verified Access Conditions governing Dog Access at a Place.
_Avoid_: Dog-Friendliness Summary, policy text, tags

**Access Area**:
The part of a Place in which dogs are permitted, such as indoors, outdoors, or a designated area only.
_Avoid_: Place category, amenity

**Restraint Condition**:
The required or permitted degree of physical control, such as leash required, off-leash permitted, or carrier required.
_Avoid_: Access Area, behavior rule

**Dog Eligibility**:
The dogs covered by an Access Condition, including any size, number, or other stated restriction.
_Avoid_: Member preference, dog profile

**Availability Window**:
The times, days, or seasons during which an Access Condition applies.
_Avoid_: Opening hours, freshness period

**Permission Requirement**:
Whether Dog Access is standing permission, requires asking on arrival, or requires advance approval.
_Avoid_: Verification, Claim

**Dog Amenity**:
A facility or service that supports visiting dogs or their handlers without determining Dog Access.
The existence of a Dog Amenity is factual Place information rather than a Dog-Friendliness Rating.
_Avoid_: Access Condition, Dog-Friendliness Rating

**Dog-Friendliness**:
The aggregated Member assessment of how welcoming, comfortable, clear, and well-equipped a Place is for dogs and their handlers.
_Avoid_: Dog Access, permission, business quality

**Dog-Friendliness Dimension**:
An independently rated aspect of Dog-Friendliness that may be marked not applicable for a particular Member experience.
_Avoid_: Access Condition, business category

**Welcome Dimension**:
The Member assessment of how positively dogs and their handlers are received at a Place.
_Avoid_: Staff rating, service quality

**Clarity Dimension**:
The Member assessment of how easy the Place's dog rules are to understand.
_Avoid_: Information accuracy, Verification

**Comfort Dimension**:
The Member assessment of whether the space and environment make visiting with a dog practical and relaxed.
_Avoid_: Safety report, accessibility audit

**Thoughtfulness Dimension**:
The Member assessment of how deliberately the Place accommodates dogs through appropriate consideration or amenities.
_Avoid_: Amenity inventory, luxury score

**Information Accuracy**:
The degree to which a Place Profile matches current conditions at its Place.
Inaccuracy belongs in a Correction or Report and is never a Dog-Friendliness Dimension.
_Avoid_: Clarity Dimension, rating criterion

**Safety Concern**:
A claim that conditions at a Place may endanger dogs or their handlers and require investigation through a Report.
_Avoid_: Comfort Dimension, low rating

**Business Quality**:
The general quality of a Place's products or services unrelated to Dog Access or Dog-Friendliness and outside Hundavænt's domain.
_Avoid_: Dog-Friendliness Dimension, venue score

## People and roles

**Visitor**:
A person browsing public Hundavænt information without signing in.
_Avoid_: Guest, anonymous user

**Member**:
A person with a Hundavænt account who can use personalized and participatory features.
_Avoid_: User, registered user

**Contributor**:
A Member who helps maintain Hundavænt information through accepted Contributions.
_Avoid_: Submitter, reporter

**Trusted Contributor**:
A Contributor with a sustained history of accurate, accepted Contributions.
_Avoid_: Power user, verified user

**Moderator**:
A person authorized by Hundavænt to investigate Contributions, verify information, and control publication.
_Avoid_: Admin, reviewer

**Venue Representative**:
A person verified as authorized to provide information for a commercial, cultural, or accommodation business.
_Avoid_: Owner, business user, venue account

## Community information

**Contribution**:
A Suggestion, Correction, or Report that a Moderator confirms improved Hundavænt information or supplied useful Evidence.
_Avoid_: Submission, user content

**Suggestion**:
A Member's proposal to add a Place that Hundavænt does not currently represent.
_Avoid_: New listing, submission

**Correction**:
A Member's proposed change to information Hundavænt already publishes.
_Avoid_: Edit, report

**Report**:
A Member's signal that published information may be wrong, unsafe, misleading, or obsolete and requires investigation.
_Avoid_: Correction, complaint

**Evidence**:
Information from a Member, Venue Representative, official source, or direct observation that supports or contradicts a specific claim about a Place.
Evidence informs Verification but is not itself Verification.
_Avoid_: Verification, proof, submission

**Verification**:
A Moderator's determination that specified information is supported by sufficient Evidence at a recorded time.
_Avoid_: Approval, validation

**Reconfirmation Due**:
The status of previously verified information whose freshness period has expired without evidence that it is wrong.
_Avoid_: Unverified, expired, invalid

**Access Dispute**:
The status of an Access Condition that a Moderator considers credibly contradicted by newer Evidence.
A disputed Access Condition is not treated as verified until the dispute is resolved.
_Avoid_: Reconfirmation Due, Report, low rating

## Member activity

**Dog-Friendliness Rating**:
A Member's structured assessment of the applicable Dog-Friendliness Dimensions experienced at a Place.
_Avoid_: Review, business rating, star review

**Dog-Friendliness Summary**:
The privacy-protected aggregate of eligible Dog-Friendliness Ratings for a Place, including differences between rating dimensions and Member opinions.
_Avoid_: Objective score, Access Dispute, review average

**Private Rating Note**:
A non-public explanation attached to a low Dog-Friendliness Rating for moderation and possible anonymized venue feedback.
_Avoid_: Comment, review text

**Check-in**:
A private, explicit record by a Member that they visited a Place.
_Avoid_: Tracking, location history

**Favourite**:
A private saved Place that a Member wants to revisit or follow.
_Avoid_: Like, bookmark

**Achievement**:
Recognition of a Member's personal exploration or useful community participation.
_Avoid_: Prize, score

## Venue participation

**Claim**:
A Venue Representative's request to establish authority to provide information for a Place without gaining automatic publication control.
_Avoid_: Ownership, takeover

**Venue Insight**:
An aggregated, privacy-protected pattern derived from Member activity or Dog-Friendliness Ratings for a claimed Place.
_Avoid_: User data, customer tracking

**Promotion**:
Clearly labeled paid visibility that cannot affect Verification, Dog-Friendliness Ratings, or organic ordering.
_Avoid_: Featured rating, sponsored verification
